<div class="i18n-en" markdown="1">

Capstone project with **PredicTx Health**, a precision-oncology company.
Our ten-person team is building the **management control plane** for their
Runbeam Harmony proxies — agents that broker medical-data interoperability
across heterogeneous clinical systems.

My work sits on the control-plane service: configuration, lifecycle,
observability. Spring Boot on the backend, with telemetry paths that the
product team can pull into their own dashboards.

_Write-up coming at the end of the semester._

<div class="i18n-zh" markdown="1">

与精准肿瘤学公司 **PredicTx Health** 合作的 Capstone 项目。我们十人团队在为他们的 Runbeam Harmony 代理服务构建 **管理控制面**——这些代理负责在异构医疗系统之间做数据互操作。

我主要负责控制面服务：配置管理、生命周期、可观测性。后端用 Spring Boot，telemetry 路径能被对方产品团队接入自家 dashboard。
